01THE BRIEF

Five days.
Four places.
One slower pace.

Northbound documents a fictional group journey through Northern Luzon, following a simple idea: the best part of a trip is often what happens between destinations.

We built this report around observation rather than a checklist. Each stop became a small study of architecture, food, landscape and the people who give a place its character.

NORTHBOUND / FIELD 04
THE MOMENT

Not every destination needs a schedule.

On the third morning, the plan was to leave before sunrise. Instead, rain arrived. We waited. Breakfast stretched into conversation, conversation stretched into an hour, and the hour became the best part of the day.

“The itinerary tells you where to go. The weather tells you how to remember it.”

Streets remember.

In Vigan, architecture wasn't an attraction separated from daily life. Tricycles, shops and families moved through the same streets that visitors came to photograph.
